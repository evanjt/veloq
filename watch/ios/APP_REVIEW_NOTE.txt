App Review note for the watch app's location background mode

Guideline 2.5.4 limits background modes to their intended purposes. The watch app declares
`location` in `WKBackgroundModes` and `UIBackgroundModes` for one visible feature:

- The athlete loads a section or route on the watch, then starts their run or ride in the
  Workout app. Veloq keeps receiving location behind it and raises a notification with a sound
  200 m before the section starts and at its end.
- Location runs only between loading a section and 500 m past its end, or until the athlete ends
  the guide. It never holds a workout session and records nothing.
- Veloq shows what it is doing: the guide screen names the loaded section and carries an End
  button.
