Veloq shows your intervals.icu training on your phone and looks for the routes and sections you repeat. The analysis runs on your device.

<b>Record</b>
Record rides, runs and other sports with GPS, Bluetooth sensors or a planned workout.

<b>Insights</b>
Short reads on your training, each opening to its numbers and how they were worked out.

<b>Maps</b>
Interactive maps with 3D terrain, route heatmaps, and light, dark and satellite styles.

<b>Routes and sections</b>
Compare every attempt on the routes and sections you repeat, then pin, trim or rename them.

<b>Charts</b>
Heart rate, power, pace, elevation and cadence on one timeline with the map.

<b>Fitness</b>
Fitness, fatigue and form with the zones drawn in, plus HRV, resting heart rate and sleep.

<b>Strength</b>
Your strength sets on a body diagram, by muscle group and exercise.

<b>Widgets</b>
Home-screen widgets for fitness and form, your last activity and your insights.

<b>Notifications</b>
A notification naming the route and any personal bests when a new activity arrives. Off until you turn it on.

<b>Backups</b>
Back up your settings and section decisions to a file, a folder, a cloud drive or a WebDAV server.

<b>Privacy</b>
Your training data stays on your device, with no analytics and no tracking. Optional push notifications store your athlete ID and a device token on our server for 30 days. Open source under Apache 2.0.

Supports cycling, running, swimming, and 20+ activity types. Try the built-in demo mode to explore without an account.

Requires an intervals.icu account for live data. Requires Android 7.0 or later. Not affiliated with intervals.icu.
