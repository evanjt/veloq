Veloq reads your intervals.icu account and draws it on your phone: activities, maps, charts, fitness and form. It also finds the routes and sections you ride more than once and lines each attempt up against the ones before it. The analysis runs on the device. Your training data stays there.

<b>Routes</b>
Veloq looks for roads you ride more than once, with nothing to mark up or set up. Attempts line up against each other, so you can see whether a climb is getting faster.

<b>Sections</b>
Shorter stretches inside those routes are detected the same way. Rank your efforts on a favourite climb and see which ones were your best.

<b>Maps</b>
3D terrain, route heatmaps and a timeline you can filter. Light, dark and satellite. Drag along a chart and your position moves across the map as you go.

<b>Charts</b>
Heart rate, power, pace, elevation and cadence, locked to one timeline. Zone distributions, training load, intensity and lap breakdowns.

<b>Fitness</b>
Fitness, fatigue and form on one chart with the zones drawn in. Track HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and weight. Power and pace curves for each sport.

<b>Insights</b>
A short read on your training each day. A new best on a section, a fitness milestone, how this week sits against last. Rest days are read as rest.

<b>Record</b>
Start a ride or run with GPS, auto-pause and kilometre splits. Trim the ends before you save. Upload as a FIT file, or queue it until you have signal. Indoor sessions go in by hand.

<b>Strength</b>
Log strength work and see which muscles it loaded, and which it did not.

<b>Widgets</b>
Put your form and your last activity on the home screen, updated in the background.

<b>Privacy</b>
Your training data stays on the device. No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Optional push notifications store a device token that expires after 30 days, with nothing personal attached.

<b>Open source</b>
Veloq is open source under Apache 2.0.

Cycling, running, swimming and 20+ activity types. Try demo mode to look around without an account.
