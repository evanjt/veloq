Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Veloq keeps your data on your device and the developer never receives it. The app fetches your fitness data directly from Intervals.icu and stores it locally for offline access. A copy leaves the device only when you turn on Automatic backup, tap Back Up Now, or export a backup yourself.

Authentication Methods

Veloq supports two ways to connect to your Intervals.icu account:

API Key Mode (Local Only)

  • Your API key is stored encrypted on your device using platform secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android KeyStore)
  • The key never leaves your device, all API calls go directly to Intervals.icu
  • No backend servers involved

OAuth Mode (Connected)

  • Uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0 authentication
  • A Cloudflare Worker proxy handles the token exchange to keep the client secret secure
  • The proxy temporarily stores a state parameter (5-minute TTL) for CSRF protection
  • The proxy holds intervals.icu's one-time sign-in code for up to 2 minutes, until your device redeems it
  • Your access token is returned directly to your device and stored in secure storage
  • The proxy does not store your access token

Data We Fetch

From Intervals.icu (with your permission):

  • Activities (routes, metrics, streams)
  • Wellness data (HRV, sleep, weight)
  • Calendar and planned workouts
  • Sport settings (zones, thresholds)

Local Storage

Data is stored on your device:

  • Credentials. Encrypted (SecureStore), retained until you disconnect
  • Activity data. Activities and GPS tracks are kept until you sign out or delete app data. Sensor streams (heart rate, power, cadence) are kept for the window chosen in Settings: 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, or everything, which is the default. Nothing deletes an activity by age
  • Preferences. Theme, map settings, retained until you clear app data

Backups

Automatic backup is off until you turn it on. When it is on, a backup is written to each of these places that applies:

  • Device backup. Google on Android, Apple iCloud on iOS, under the phone's own backup setting
  • Nextcloud or WebDAV server. When you set one up
  • A folder you pick. When you pick one
  • Export. Hands a backup to the share sheet, where you choose where it goes

Back Up Now writes a backup whatever the Automatic backup switch says. A backup holds your settings, including your Intervals.icu athlete ID and, if you set one, your export home location, the names of your routes, the sections you named, cut or edited with their map lines, and your pins, excludes and section history. It holds no credentials, activities, GPS tracks or sensor streams. A backup is not encrypted.

Turning Automatic backup off stops new backups. Copies already written stay where they are until you delete them there, and the last one stays in the device backup until you clear app data.

Third-Party Services

Intervals.icu

Your fitness data source. See their Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Map Tile Providers

Standard web requests, no personal data sent:

  • Base and dark map: OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap (tiles.openfreemap.org)
  • Satellite imagery: EOX Sentinel-2 (tiles.maps.eox.at), and by region swisstopo (wmts.geo.admin.ch), IGN France (data.geopf.fr), USGS (imagery.nationalmap.gov), IGN Spain (www.ign.es), Vienna (maps.wien.gv.at), PDOK (service.pdok.nl), CUZK (ags.cuzk.gov.cz), GUGiK (mapy.geoportal.gov.pl) and Luxembourg (wmts1-4.geoportail.lu)
  • 3D terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (s3.amazonaws.com)

Cloudflare Workers (OAuth mode only)

  • Temporarily stores OAuth state parameters (5-minute TTL)
  • If you opt in to push notifications: stores your athlete ID and an Expo push token (30-day TTL)
  • Stores your IP address as a rate-limit counter for 60 seconds
  • Records each webhook event handled (athlete ID, event type, activity ID) for 5 minutes, so a re-delivered event is not pushed twice
  • Does not store access tokens

Expo Push Service (notifications only)

  • Routes push notifications from our server to Apple (APNs) or Google (FCM) on your behalf
  • Sees only the push token and a minimal payload (event type, activity ID and your intervals.icu athlete ID, which the app uses to check the push is for the signed-in athlete)
  • Does not see notification content, all insight text is generated on your device

Push Notifications (Optional)

Push notifications are strictly opt-in. They are disabled by default and only available to OAuth (connected) users. API key users are never prompted and no data is ever sent.

What is stored when you opt in

  • Your athlete ID and an Expo push token are stored on auth.veloq.fit (Cloudflare KV) with automatic 30-day expiry
  • This is the only data stored on our server, no activity data, GPS, fitness metrics, or personal information
  • Your push token is re-registered each time you open the app (refreshing the 30-day TTL)

How notifications work

  • When Intervals.icu detects a new activity, it sends a webhook event to auth.veloq.fit
  • Our server relays a minimal push message (containing only the event type, activity ID and your intervals.icu athlete ID, which the app uses to check the push is for the signed-in athlete) through the Expo Push Service
  • Expo routes the message through Apple (APNs) or Google (FCM) to reach your device
  • Your device generates the notification content (PR details, insights) entirely on-device, neither our server, Expo, Apple, nor Google see this content

Disabling notifications

You can disable notifications at any time in Settings. Disabling sends a removal request to our server. If the request fails (e.g. no internet), the app retries on next open. Logging out also removes your token when the request reaches the server; if it cannot (e.g. no internet), the app tells you, and this device may keep receiving notifications until the server data expires. In all cases, server data expires automatically after 30 days.

No analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.

Data Sharing

None. The developer has no access to your data. Data flows between your device and Intervals.icu, the map tile servers named above (no identifying data) and, only when you turn them on, push notifications and the backup destinations named above.

Security

  • Credentials encrypted using platform secure storage
  • HTTPS/TLS for all network requests
  • OAuth state validation (CSRF protection)
  • Webhook requests verified against a shared secret
  • No background telemetry or data collection

Disclaimer

Veloq is an independent, open-source project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Intervals.icu in any way. For information about how Intervals.icu handles your data, please refer to their privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Not intended for users under 13. No data is collected from any user.

Your Rights

  • View your data: All data is visible within the app
  • Delete your data: Uninstall the app or clear app data in device settings. A backup on a server, in a folder or in the device backup is deleted there
  • Revoke access: Disconnect your account in app settings, or revoke the OAuth app in Intervals.icu settings
  • Server data removal: If you uninstall the app with notifications enabled, your push token and athlete ID expire automatically after 30 days. For immediate removal, contact developer@veloq.fit
