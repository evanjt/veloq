Delete your Veloq data

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Veloq keeps your training data on your phone. Its server holds only a push record, and these steps remove it.

How to delete your data

  • In the app: turn notifications off in Veloq's settings, or sign out. Either removes your push record from Veloq's server at once.
  • By email: write to developer@veloq.fit from any address, with your intervals.icu athlete id, and the record is removed by hand.
  • On your phone: uninstalling Veloq, or clearing its data in the device settings, removes everything it holds on the phone.

What Veloq's server holds

The server holds your intervals.icu athlete id and your push token. The steps above delete both, and in any case they expire 30 days after you last opened the app. Short-lived records expire on their own: sign-in state after 5 minutes, a rate-limit counter after 60 seconds, and a handled webhook after 5 minutes.

What is not Veloq's to delete

Your training data lives in your intervals.icu account. Backups live in your own iCloud, Google Drive or folder. Delete them there.
