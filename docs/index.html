Your training,
visualised

Your fitness, form and repeated routes, read from your intervals.icu account and analysed on your phone.

No account is needed to read this site, and Veloq has no account of its own. The app shows your intervals.icu training, or demo data with no account at all.

On Android, optional backups can go to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only Veloq can open. Privacy Policy

View your data

Access your intervals.icu data on mobile with offline support.

⏱

Record

Record rides, runs and other sports with GPS, auto-pause and splits in your own units. Pair Bluetooth heart rate, power and cadence sensors, or follow a planned workout from your intervals.icu calendar with its targets on screen. Trim the ends before you save, then upload a FIT file or queue it until you have signal. Indoor sessions can be entered by hand. Start a recording from a home-screen widget, Control Centre or Siri, and follow it on the Lock Screen.

💡

Insights

A short read on your training: section records and trends, fitness milestones, how this week compares with last, HRV trends and strength progression. Each insight opens to show its numbers and how they were worked out.

🌎

Maps

Interactive maps with 3D terrain, route heatmaps and a timeline you can filter, in light, dark and satellite styles. Drag along a chart and your position moves across the map.

🛠

Routes and sections

Veloq looks for the routes and sections you repeat, with nothing to set up, and cuts them the same way on every device. A section page compares every attempt: the trend, the spread of your times and the line coloured where time was won or lost. Pin, trim, extend or rename a section, and every change keeps a history you can revert.

📈

Charts

Heart rate, power, pace, elevation and cadence on one timeline with the map. Zone distributions, training load, intensity and lap breakdowns.

💪

Fitness

Fitness, fatigue and form on one chart with the zones drawn in. HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and wellness, and power and pace curves for each sport.

🏋

Strength

The strength sets in your activities, drawn on a body diagram: the muscle groups each session worked, the balance between opposing groups and the progression of each exercise.

📱

Widgets

Home-screen widgets for your fitness and form, your last activity, your latest insights and your strength work, refreshed in the background.

🔔

Notifications

When intervals.icu receives a new activity, Veloq can send a notification naming the route and any personal bests, in your language. Notifications stay off until you turn them on.

💾

Backups

Back up your settings and section decisions to a small file, by hand or automatically. Keep it in your device's own backup, or add a WebDAV server, a folder or a cloud drive. A restore fetches your activities again and applies your decisions where they match.

🔒

Privacy

Your training data stays on your device. No analytics, no tracking. Optional push notifications store your intervals.icu athlete ID and a device token on our server, deleted automatically after 30 days. Open source under Apache 2.0.
