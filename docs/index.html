View your data

Access your intervals.icu data on mobile with offline support.

🌎

Maps

View your activities on interactive maps with 3D terrain, route heatmaps, and timeline filtering. Scrub through charts and watch your position update in real-time.

📈

Charts

View heart rate, power, pace, elevation, and cadence charts from your intervals.icu data.

🛠

Route tracking

Automatically detect repeated routes and sections. Compare efforts over time to see your progress on favourite climbs and stretches.

💪

Fitness

View your CTL/ATL/TSB fitness data and form zones as calculated by intervals.icu.

📷

On-Device

Route and section detection runs entirely on your device.

🔒

Privacy

Your data stays on your device and the developer never receives it. Push notifications and backups are off until you turn them on. No analytics, no tracking. Open source.

Getting Started

Connect to your intervals.icu account in three simple steps.

1

Install Veloq

Get it on the App Store or Google Play

2

Sign in with intervals.icu

Tap Sign in with intervals.icu and allow access. Or tap Use API key instead and paste your API key from intervals.icu Settings → Developer Settings.

3

Start exploring

Your activities, fitness and routes load onto your phone and are analysed there.
